01 / ABOUT

A LITTLE LOUDER

One crew.
One sweaty room.

If you're craving a musical adventure as delightful as a fresh papaya on a summer day, you've found it. Papaya Ryders are a band born from the cultural crossroads of Germany, Austria and Italy — and they call their sound "Papayadelic."

Founded in Munich, the band weave indie, funk, blues and rock into something uniquely their own: unusual compositions, captivating live shows, and a living, breathing lineup — drums, bass and guitars at the core, colored by keys, harmonica, vocals and whatever the night calls for. One moment you're lost in an atmospheric passage; the next, swept up in a whirlwind of rock and funk that gets the whole room moving.
